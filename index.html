Questions

Is Pinfo free?

Yes. Browsing, saving, RSVPs, chat and hosting your first event are free. Hosting more than one event at a time needs a subscription (1.99 EUR a month).

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse the map. To RSVP, chat or host, sign in with your phone number - no password.

Which cities does Pinfo cover?

Pinfo lists events in 99 cities, most of them in Europe, and anyone can host an event anywhere.

Where do the events come from?

From public event listings and from people hosting on Pinfo. Every event page links to its source.

How do private events work?

Only the people you invite can see a private event. Invite them from your contacts or send a personal link; you see who is going, who might come and who hasn't answered yet.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet - Pinfo is on iPhone for now.

How is my data handled?

We only use what the app needs to work. Read the privacy policy for the details.
